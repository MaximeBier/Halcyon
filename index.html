Why an analog overlay?

Classic keyboard overlays show a key as on or off. Yours is analog: show what it can actually do.

Pressure, not just clicks

Each key fills with press depth and switches color at actuation. The stream shows half-presses, feathering, releases: everything an on/off overlay misses.

OBS · connected

Hooked to OBS in 3 steps

A wizard walks you through: the keyboard is detected on its own, OBS connects with a copy-paste URL, and you add keys simply by pressing them. No files to edit.

Your style, down to the key

Colors, fill direction, corner radius, font, size, opacity: everything is configurable, globally or per key. Arrange keys freely on the canvas and show only the ones that matter.

localhost · 0 cloud

Local and lightweight

Everything runs on your machine: no data sent to the internet, no account. The overlay is a plain browser source, negligible for your FPS in game and on stream.
